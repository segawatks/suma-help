Magic Tank

Once you get a tank and move a fish into it, you'll see a screen like this.

Overview The Magic Tank lets you store a fish you're raising for a while. For example, move your clownfish into the tank, then buy a chinstrap penguin at the shop. You can swap the penguin and the clownfish back and forth and raise them in turns. You can get up to 5 tanks.
Magic Food Once a day, you can give Magic Food to each fish in a tank to help it grow a little. Magic Food is randomly chosen from 3 types, each with its own drop rate and growth rate.
You can refill Magic Food in these ways:
・Watch an ad: +15
・Purchase: +150
・Subscribe: unlimited

Even if you don't feed them, your fish won't lose growth or get weaker.

Once the growth bar reaches 100%, the fish can't grow any more in the tank. Move it back to the sea to keep raising it.

In this guide, "fish" also includes penguins and other creatures.

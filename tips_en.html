Lock Screen Widget

Check your timer without unlocking your iPhone.

Lock your iPhone, then touch and hold the Lock Screen.

Tap "Add Widget".

Find "Focus & Fish" in the list and tap it.

There are two widgets, large and small. Choose the one you like.

Finally, don't forget to tap "Done" at the top right.

Home Screen Widget

Touch and hold the app icon on your Home Screen to turn it into a Home Screen widget.

The widget shows the time left on your timer and the time left until the deadlines you set on the Chart screen.

Schedule

Turn on "Focus Time Schedule" in the Control Panel and open "Schedule Settings".
Here you can set Focus Time to start automatically on any day and at any time you choose.
From "Schedule-specific Settings", you can set blocked apps and a default Focus Time length just for scheduled sessions.
